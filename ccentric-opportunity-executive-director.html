About the Role

A mission-driven community health organisation is seeking an Executive Director to lead it through its next stage of growth. Reporting to the board, you'll balance the organisation's community purpose with the commercial discipline needed to build a sustainable, high-performing organisation.

This is a genuine opportunity to shape the future direction of an organisation that makes a real difference to the community it serves, while strengthening its governance, funding base and operational capability.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead organisational strategy in partnership with the board
  • Build and diversify funding and revenue streams
  • Strengthen governance, risk management and compliance
  • Lead, develop and support the staff team and culture
  • Represent the organisation with funders, government and community partners
  • Ensure services remain responsive to community need

About You

  • Senior leadership experience in a not-for-profit or community health setting
  • Strong commercial and financial acumen
  • Experience working with and reporting to a board
  • Genuine commitment to community health and mission-driven work
  • Excellent relationship-building and communication skills
