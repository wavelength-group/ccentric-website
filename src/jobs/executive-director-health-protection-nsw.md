## About the NSW Ministry of Health

Australia's health system is considered one of the best in the world, yet, like other health systems, we continue to face complex and evolving public health challenges. To meet these head-on, the [NSW Ministry of Health](https://www.health.nsw.gov.au/) works collaboratively across the health system, including pillar organisations, local health districts, specialty health networks and primary care practitioners, including Aboriginal health practitioners, to protect and improve the health and wellbeing of communities across New South Wales.

## The Role

The Executive Director, Health Protection NSW (HPNSW) will lead the state's response in preventing, reducing and controlling infectious diseases and responding to environmental health risks. Reporting to the Chief Health Officer and Deputy Secretary, Population and Public Health, the Executive Director will provide senior executive leadership across HPNSW and partner closely with public health units, sexual health units, Aboriginal Community Controlled Services, primary care and external government agencies.

The role provides authoritative, evidence-based public health advice and technical support to high-level stakeholders, including the Chief Health Officer, the Minister for Health and the broader health system, regarding disease control, environmental risk management and epidemiological insights.

The Executive Director is a key spokesperson for NSW Health on matters relating to infectious diseases and environmental health, and must bring a confident public persona, sound judgement, integrity, excellent communication skills and the ability to be a source of trusted health advice.

## About You

We are seeking a dynamic and outstanding individual with strong leadership, superior communication skills and extensive experience in public health medicine to fill this executive leadership role.

A motivated and inspirational leader, you will nurture a high functioning team and foster strong foundations of a supportive and collaborative culture to deliver health outcomes for the people of NSW and contribute to national capability.

It is an exciting opportunity for an experienced senior leader who has:

- Current specialist registration (or eligibility for specialist registration) as a Medical Practitioner with the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (AHPRA)
- Fellowship of the Australasian Faculty of Public Health Medicine (Royal Australasian College of Physicians) or relevant specialist qualification
- Extensive experience in public health medicine practice, such as policy and program development, epidemiological investigations, research commissioning, emergency response and health system implementation, that will command credibility with stakeholders
- A strong and demonstrated ability to understand and operate within a complex government and multi-agency environment
