## About the Role

A leading health faculty is seeking a Head of School to lead its Nursing & Midwifery discipline. This role carries responsibility for academic strategy, research direction, teaching quality and staff development, balancing scholarly leadership with the practical realities of running a large academic school.

You'll work closely with the Dean and Pro Vice-Chancellor on institutional strategy, while maintaining strong relationships with clinical placement partners and professional accreditation bodies.

## Key Responsibilities

- Lead academic strategy and direction for the School of Nursing & Midwifery
- Oversee research output, higher degree supervision and grant strategy
- Ensure teaching quality and curriculum accreditation standards
- Manage academic staff performance, development and succession
- Represent the school with industry, clinical and professional bodies
- Contribute to broader faculty and institutional strategy

## About You

- Professorial-level academic standing with a strong research record
- Experience in academic leadership and staff management
- Understanding of higher education governance and accreditation
- Background in nursing or midwifery highly regarded
- Strong stakeholder and relationship management skills
