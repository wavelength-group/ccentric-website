## About Forensicare

[Forensicare](https://www.forensicare.vic.gov.au/) is Victoria's statewide specialist forensic mental health provider. Around 1,100 people care for people with serious mental illness who are connected to, or at risk of contact with, the justice system, across Thomas Embling Hospital, Victoria's prisons and growing community services.

It is an organisation on the move: a $515.7 million Thomas Embling expansion adding 82 secure beds, expanding community services, new models of care, and a new Executive Director, Clinical Services with a mandate for change. A research partnership with Swinburne University of Technology keeps it at the leading edge.

## The Role

As one of only three Directors of Clinical Services, you will lead the psychiatrists delivering specialist care across 14 of Victoria's 15 adult prisons: around 26 consultants (about 14 FTE) and about 20 registrar FTE. This is a workforce and operations leadership role held by a consultant psychiatrist. You will:

- Make the allocation, cover and prioritisation calls that keep services safe across multiple sites
- Own clinical governance, quality and safety for the directorate
- Build strong partnerships with Corrections Victoria, Justice Health, the Courts and the Chief Psychiatrist
- Shape the future of the service as a member of the Senior Executive Leadership Group, reporting to the Executive Director, Clinical Services and working closely with the Executive Director, Prison Operations

This role will have real influence, a genuine build phase with backing to lead change, and a 9-day fortnight with one day structured as private practice, which you can use as a non-clinical day or to keep your hand in clinically on a unit.

## About You

We are seeking a psychiatrist and people leader with:

- Fellowship of the Royal Australian and New Zealand College of Psychiatrists (or equivalent), and registration, or eligibility for registration, as a medical practitioner in Victoria
- Clinical experience in forensic mental health, including in correctional settings, and a sound grasp of clinical risk
- Proven leadership of a medical workforce, with expertise in clinical governance, quality and risk management
- Senior leadership experience in a complex health service, and the operational judgement to make timely calls

Experienced consultants ready for their first directorship are encouraged to apply.
