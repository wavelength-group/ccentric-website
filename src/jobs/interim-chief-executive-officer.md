## About the Role

A residential aged care provider is seeking an experienced Interim Chief Executive Officer to provide steady, capable leadership through a period of organisational transition. This is a hands-on role for a leader who can quickly build trust with the board, staff, residents and families while maintaining operational and regulatory stability.

You'll be expected to stabilise operations, support the organisation through to a permanent leadership appointment, and leave the business in a strong position for its next chapter.

## Key Responsibilities

- Provide immediate, steady executive leadership across the organisation
- Maintain compliance with aged care quality and safety standards
- Support the board through governance and transition planning
- Stabilise and reassure staff, residents and families during the transition
- Oversee day-to-day operations and financial performance
- Support recruitment and handover to a permanent CEO

## About You

- Proven CEO or senior executive experience in aged care or health services
- Strong understanding of aged care regulatory and quality frameworks
- Comfortable stepping into complex, sensitive transitions
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
- Available for an interim engagement at short notice
