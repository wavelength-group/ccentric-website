## About the Role

A large acute care network is seeking a Director of Nursing to lead nursing and midwifery practice across its sites. Reporting to the Executive Director of Clinical Services, you'll set the standard for clinical care, workforce planning and professional development across the nursing workforce.

This role carries real influence over patient care standards and staff culture, working closely with clinical directors, unit managers and the executive team.

## Key Responsibilities

- Lead nursing and midwifery strategy, standards and professional practice
- Oversee nursing workforce planning, recruitment and retention
- Drive clinical quality, patient safety and care standards
- Partner with clinical directors and unit managers across all sites
- Represent nursing leadership at the executive and board level
- Support education, training and career development pathways

## About You

- Registered Nurse with current AHPRA registration
- Substantial experience in senior nursing leadership
- Strong understanding of clinical governance and workforce planning
- Demonstrated ability to lead through change
- Excellent interpersonal and stakeholder management skills
