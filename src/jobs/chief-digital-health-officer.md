## About the Role

A multi-site hospital network is seeking a Chief Digital Health Officer to lead its digital transformation and health technology strategy. This is a newly elevated executive role, reflecting the growing importance of digital capability to clinical care, patient experience and operational efficiency.

You'll bridge the gap between clinical need and technology delivery, working across IT, clinical leadership and executive stakeholders to shape a multi-year digital roadmap.

## Key Responsibilities

- Set and lead the digital health strategy and technology roadmap
- Oversee electronic medical record and clinical systems programs
- Partner with clinical leaders to translate technology into better patient outcomes
- Manage cybersecurity, data governance and digital risk
- Lead digital and IT teams through significant transformation programs
- Represent digital strategy at executive and board level

## About You

- Significant experience leading digital or IT strategy in healthcare
- Strong understanding of clinical systems and health information standards
- Track record delivering large-scale technology transformation
- Excellent stakeholder management across clinical and technical teams
- Strategic mindset balanced with hands-on delivery experience
