## The Opportunity

- Lead an established, privately-owned radiology service while maintaining your own clinical practice
- Hybrid model with a minimum two days per week on-site, allowing regional or city living
- Step into clinical leadership, with strong support to grow in the role
- Seven-figure remuneration package

## About the Role

[Keystone Radiology](https://keystoneradiology.com.au/) is a respected, independent, radiologist-led provider delivering exceptional imaging services across major regional health services, including Goulburn Valley Health and Grampians Health. They are seeking an experienced FRANZCR-qualified radiologist to step into the role of Clinical Director of Radiology at their Shepparton operation, based within [Goulburn Valley Health](https://gvhealth.org.au/).

This is a rare opportunity to combine high-level clinical practice with leadership across a comprehensive imaging service spanning CT, MRI, ultrasound, X-ray and interventional radiology, while maintaining your own clinical practice. Working closely with senior clinicians, hospital executives and medical imaging teams, you will drive service excellence, advance quality improvement and help shape the future direction of the service.

With genuine flexibility built in, including the ability to work remotely for part of the week, the role suits radiologists in Melbourne, regional Victoria or southern New South Wales seeking a leadership opportunity without sacrificing work-life balance.

## What's on Offer

- Seven-figure earning potential with additional incentive payments
- Clinical Director, regional and relocation allowances, or a hybrid model with modern, self-contained accommodation provided
- A diverse and rewarding hospital caseload
- Strong support from Keystone's experienced radiologist leadership team
- Scope to influence strategy, innovation and service development
- Visible impact on healthcare outcomes across regional Victoria

## About You

- FRANZCR-qualified with current AHPRA registration
- Strong clinical skills across a broad imaging caseload
- A genuine interest in leadership, service development and mentoring others
- A collaborative approach with clinicians, executives and imaging teams
- Ready to take on a first formal leadership role, or to build on existing leadership experience
