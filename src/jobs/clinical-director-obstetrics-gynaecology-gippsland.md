## The Opportunity

An outstanding opportunity exists for an experienced FRANZCOG-qualified Obstetrician and Gynaecologist to take on a unique dual-site leadership position across Bairnsdale Regional Health Service and Latrobe Regional Health. This newly established role combines strategic clinical leadership with a substantive consultant practice, providing the opportunity to influence maternity and women's health services across the Gippsland region.

As Clinical Director, you will provide governance and leadership for the Level 3 maternity service at Bairnsdale Regional Health Service, supporting a GP Obstetrician model and ensuring safe, high-quality maternity care, robust clinical governance and strong integration with tertiary referral services. Concurrently, you will maintain a consultant obstetrics and gynaecology practice at Latrobe Regional Health, Gippsland's designated Level 5 maternity service and regional referral centre.

This is a rare opportunity to shape contemporary models of maternity care, strengthen regional clinical networks, mentor the next generation of clinicians and drive service development across both organisations. The successful candidate will play a key role in workforce sustainability, quality improvement, clinical governance and strategic service planning while delivering high-quality obstetric and gynaecological care.

## About You

You will be a collaborative and respected clinical leader with:

- Fellowship of the Royal Australian and New Zealand College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists (FRANZCOG).
- Specialist registration with AHPRA.
- Demonstrated leadership experience within obstetrics and gynaecology services.
- Experience in clinical governance, quality improvement and maternity service management.
- Strong understanding of rural and regional healthcare delivery.
- A commitment to education, workforce development and multidisciplinary collaboration.

## Why Gippsland?

This is an opportunity to combine meaningful clinical leadership with an exceptional regional lifestyle. Gippsland offers diverse communities, outstanding natural environments, affordable living and easy access to Melbourne, while allowing you to make a genuine impact on women's health services across an entire region.

Join two progressive regional health services at a pivotal time and help shape the future of maternity and women's healthcare across Gippsland.
