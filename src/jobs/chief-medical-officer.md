## About the Role

A major metropolitan health service is seeking an experienced Chief Medical Officer to lead clinical strategy, medical governance and quality across its network. This is a senior executive role reporting directly to the Chief Executive, working closely with the board and executive team to shape the clinical direction of the organisation.

You'll provide clinical leadership across a broad and complex health service, balancing operational delivery with long-term strategic planning, workforce development and patient safety.

## Key Responsibilities

- Provide strategic clinical leadership across all medical services
- Lead medical governance, credentialing and clinical risk frameworks
- Partner with the executive team on service planning and organisational strategy
- Represent the organisation with regulators, universities and external stakeholders
- Mentor and develop senior medical staff and clinical directors
- Champion quality, safety and continuous improvement in patient care

## About You

- Fellowship of a relevant specialist medical college
- Significant experience in senior medical leadership roles
- Strong track record in clinical governance and health service management
- Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills
- Current AHPRA registration
