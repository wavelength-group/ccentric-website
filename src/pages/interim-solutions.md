## When an interim leader is the right call

Not every leadership need can wait six months for a permanent search. An interim or fixed-term appointment gives you an experienced leader quickly, for exactly as long as you need one. It is often the right move when:

- a senior role falls vacant suddenly and the work cannot stop;
- you need cover for parental, medical or long-service leave;
- a transformation, turnaround or integration needs dedicated leadership;
- a defined project has to be delivered, such as a system implementation, an accreditation programme or a service redesign;
- you want to stabilise a role and scope it properly before making a permanent appointment; or
- a board or regulator expects leadership capacity to be in place immediately.

## The benefits

- **Speed.** An interim leader can be in place in days or weeks, not the months a permanent appointment takes.
- **Proven expertise from day one.** You engage someone who has done the job before and adds value immediately, without a long learning curve.
- **Objectivity.** An experienced interim brings an outside perspective, is free of internal politics, and is willing to make the difficult calls a situation sometimes needs.
- **Continuity.** The right person keeps a team and its work steady through a transition, protecting momentum rather than letting it stall.
- **Flexibility.** You engage them for exactly as long as the need lasts, with a clean, planned exit at the end.
- **Lower risk.** Interim cover buys you the time to stabilise, understand the role properly, and make a considered permanent appointment rather than a rushed one.
- **A stronger team at handover.** A good interim leader documents the work, transfers knowledge, and leaves the team in better shape than they found it.

## Interim or fixed-term?

The two overlap, and we help you choose the right fit. An **interim** appointment is usually immediate, senior and outcome-focused, often engaged at a day rate to steady a situation or lead through change. A **fixed-term** appointment is a defined contract for a known period, well suited to a specific project or a planned period of cover. In both cases the principle is the same: the right experienced leader, for exactly as long as you need them.
