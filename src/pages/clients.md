## How we work

Ccentric works on a retained basis, as a search partner rather than a contingent supplier. We commit to a mandate, map the relevant market thoroughly, approach leaders directly and confidentially, and present a shortlist we stand behind. The result is a smaller number of genuinely suitable candidates, assessed properly, rather than a high volume of applications to sift through.

## Our process

**1. Understanding the mandate.** We start by understanding the role, the organisation, its leadership context and the outcome you need, not just the position description.

**2. Search strategy and market mapping.** We map the relevant market systematically, identifying and researching the leaders who fit, including those who are not actively looking.

**3. Confidential approach.** We approach candidates directly and discreetly, representing your opportunity credibly and protecting your organisation's position in the market.

**4. Assessment.** We assess candidates against the brief through structured interviews and, where appropriate, formal assessment and referencing, so your shortlist is genuinely comparable.

**5. Shortlist and presentation.** We present a considered shortlist with honest, detailed insight on each candidate, not a long list to work through.

**6. Interviews and selection.** We coordinate and support your interview process, gather structured feedback, and help you reach a confident decision.

**7. References, offer and negotiation.** We manage referencing and act as an honest broker through the offer and negotiation stage, to a fair outcome for both sides.

**8. Onboarding and aftercare.** We stay engaged after the appointment, because a successful search is measured by how the leader performs six and twelve months in, not just on the day they start.

## Why organisations choose Ccentric

- **Sector expertise.** We know healthcare and the broader health economy, the leaders in it, and the pressures you face.
- **Discretion.** Senior search depends on trust, and we handle every mandate confidentially.
- **Rigour and honesty.** We assess thoroughly and advise candidly, including when the honest answer is not the easy one.
- **Partnership.** We act as trusted advisers, and we measure success by the long-term outcome of an appointment.
- **Reach.** As part of the Wavelength Group, we draw on deep networks across the health workforce, and on international partners where a mandate calls for it.
