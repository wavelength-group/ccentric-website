## Who we are

Ccentric is a specialist executive search and leadership advisory firm focused on healthcare, life sciences and the organisations that serve the health economy. We are retained to appoint chief executives, chief medical officers, directors of medical services, clinical directors, executives and board members, and to advise on leadership capability, assessment and succession.

Our work sits at the intersection of clinical understanding and commercial judgement. We know the sector, the leaders in it, and the pressures organisations are navigating, which is what allows us to find people who fit not just the role, but the context.

## Part of the Wavelength Group

Ccentric is part of the Wavelength Group, a health workforce solutions group that has operated for more than 25 years with offices in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and the Gold Coast. The group brings together specialist medical recruitment, regulatory and migration services and executive search, giving Ccentric unusual depth of reach across the health workforce, from frontline clinicians to boards.

## Our values

- **Expertise.** Sector knowledge is our differentiator. We invest in understanding the clinical, operational and governance realities our clients face.
- **Discretion.** Senior search depends on trust. We handle every engagement, and every candidate, with care and confidentiality.
- **Partnership.** We act as trusted advisers, not transactional suppliers, and we measure success by the long-term outcome of an appointment.
- **Rigour.** We assess thoroughly and advise honestly, even when the honest answer is not the easy one.
